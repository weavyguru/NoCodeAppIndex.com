Webflow FAQ

Do I need coding knowledge to use Webflow?

No.

Does Webflow have database integration?

Native, but mostly CMS/content-oriented rather than a general relational app database. Great for content and forms, less ideal for complex app data models.

Does Webflow support A/B testing?

Native. Webflow Optimize provides experimentation/personalization capabilities.

Does Webflow support ecommerce?

Native. Webflow has its own ecommerce product.

Does Webflow support ads?

Third-party/manual.

Does Webflow support SEO?

Native and strong. SEO is one of Webflow’s biggest strengths.

Does Webflow include analytics?

Native. Webflow Analyze is first-party.

Can I build by typing a prompt in Webflow?

Yes. Webflow AI can generate sites from prompts.

Can I edit an existing project with a prompt in Webflow?

Partial. AI helps, but visual editing remains central.

Do I need a custom domain to go public with Webflow?

No. You can publish to a staging Webflow URL first; a custom domain is optional for the branded version.

How does publishing work in Webflow?

Publish to a webflow.io staging URL or to your own custom domain with a site plan.

Can I build video games with Webflow?

No.

Can I build surveys that save responses with Webflow?

Yes. Forms can save submissions, but Webflow is not a full survey platform or app backend.

Can I build multi-user web apps with Webflow?

Limited/partial. User Accounts and memberships exist, but full SaaS-style app logic is not the core product.

Can I share Webflow projects or apps easily?

Easy.
