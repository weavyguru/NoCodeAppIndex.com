Durable FAQ

Do I need coding knowledge to use Durable?

No.

Does Durable have database integration?

Limited/native only for business-style data such as leads, contacts, or CRM records. It is not a general app database platform.

Does Durable support A/B testing?

No official native A/B testing feature found in the docs reviewed.

Does Durable support ecommerce?

Partial/official. Durable supports payments and lighter ecommerce-style use cases, but it is not a deep commerce platform.

Does Durable support ads?

No official native ad tooling found.

Does Durable support SEO?

Native. Automated SEO is one of Durable’s headline selling points.

Does Durable include analytics?

Native basic analytics, plus integration options such as Google Analytics.

Can I build by typing a prompt in Durable?

Yes.

Can I edit an existing project with a prompt in Durable?

Partial. AI assistance is available, but editing also relies on the site builder/dashboard.

Do I need a custom domain to go public with Durable?

No. Durable gives you a hosted subdomain first; custom domains are optional on paid plans.

How does publishing work in Durable?

Generate a site, then publish through Durable-hosted infrastructure. Paid plans are generally needed for full production publishing.

Can I build video games with Durable?

No.

Can I build surveys that save responses with Durable?

Yes for lead forms and simple response collection.

Can I build multi-user web apps with Durable?

Limited/no for true multi-user apps. Durable is more website plus business tools than app platform.

Can I share Durable projects or apps easily?

Easy for public websites.
