Softr FAQ

Do I need coding knowledge to use Softr?

No.

Does Softr have database integration?

Native or external. Softr Databases are available, and you can also connect external data sources.

Does Softr support A/B testing?

No official native A/B testing feature found in the docs reviewed.

Does Softr support ecommerce?

Official payments support through Stripe checkout, billing, and portals, but not a full enterprise commerce suite.

Does Softr support ads?

Third-party/manual.

Does Softr support SEO?

Native. Softr includes SEO settings and guidance for public apps/sites.

Does Softr include analytics?

Official integration rather than deep native analytics. Google Analytics is supported.

Can I build by typing a prompt in Softr?

Yes. AI Co-Builder can generate apps from prompts.

Can I edit an existing project with a prompt in Softr?

Yes. Prompt-based changes are supported.

Do I need a custom domain to go public with Softr?

No. You can publish publicly on a Softr URL first; custom domains are optional.

How does publishing work in Softr?

One-click publish to a Softr-hosted URL, with optional custom domains and permissions settings.

Can I build video games with Softr?

No or not ideal. Softr is much more a business app platform than a game platform.

Can I build surveys that save responses with Softr?

Yes.

Can I build multi-user web apps with Softr?

Yes. Portals, permissions, and user groups are a core strength.

Can I share Softr projects or apps easily?

Easy.
