Framer FAQ

Do I need coding knowledge to use Framer?

No.

Does Framer have database integration?

No general native app database. Framer has CMS, forms, and external integrations, but not a built-in full app backend.

Does Framer support A/B testing?

Native/official through Framer Convert and related testing functionality.

Does Framer support ecommerce?

Third-party/manual.

Does Framer support ads?

Third-party/manual.

Does Framer support SEO?

Native and strong. Framer includes sitemap and robots support plus page-level SEO controls.

Does Framer include analytics?

Native.

Can I build by typing a prompt in Framer?

Yes.

Can I edit an existing project with a prompt in Framer?

Partial. Prompting helps, but visual editing is still central.

Do I need a custom domain to go public with Framer?

No. A Framer-hosted URL is available first; custom domains are optional.

How does publishing work in Framer?

Publish to a Framer-hosted URL or connect a custom domain.

Can I build video games with Framer?

No.

Can I build surveys that save responses with Framer?

Yes. Native forms can send data by email, webhook, or Google Sheets.

Can I build multi-user web apps with Framer?

Limited. Good for collaboration and some gated experiences, but not a full SaaS backend.

Can I share Framer projects or apps easily?

Easy.
