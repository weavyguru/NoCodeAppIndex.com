Who is Gainable best for?

Ops, sales, finance, and founder teams that want to turn spreadsheets or CRM data into live, multi-user internal apps with dashboards and KPIs, without coding or assembling a backend.

Main caveat

It targets authenticated internal and business apps, not public sites or storefronts, so there is no SEO, native ecommerce, ads, or A/B testing, and published apps live on a gainable.app subdomain unless you are on the enterprise tier.

Gainable FAQ

Do I need coding knowledge to use Gainable?

No for basic use. Apps are built and refined in plain English by the planner and build agents, though developers can optionally drive it with the gaia CLI or a code mode for hand-authoring.

Does Gainable have database integration?

Native. Every Gainable app ships with a built-in database that auto-saves submissions, persists data across users and sessions, backs up regularly, and exposes API endpoints.

Does Gainable support A/B testing?

No official native A/B testing feature found in the sources reviewed. Use external experimentation tools if you need traffic-splitting tests.

Does Gainable support ecommerce?

No native checkout or payments. Stripe connects only as a read-only data source, so a real store still needs custom work or an outside product.

Does Gainable support ads?

No official native ad tooling found in the sources reviewed. Ads are outside the scope of authenticated internal apps.

Does Gainable support SEO?

Limited. Gainable apps are authenticated, multi-user internal tools published to a gainable.app subdomain, so public SEO features such as metadata and sitemaps are not part of the product.

Does Gainable include analytics?

Native. Gainable generates dashboards and KPI cards from your business data, though this is business analytics rather than built-in end-user product analytics.

Can I build by typing a prompt in Gainable?

Yes. Describing what you want in plain English is the core workflow; a planner agent breaks it down and a build agent generates the app.

Can I edit an existing project with a prompt in Gainable?

Yes. You can refine an existing app conversationally, adding columns, views, and routes with follow-up prompts.

Do I need a custom domain to go public with Gainable?

No. One-click publish deploys to a Gainable-hosted slug.gainable.app URL; a custom domain is only needed at the enterprise tier.

How does publishing work in Gainable?

One-click publish, from the web app or via gaia publish, deploys to a hosted gainable.app subdomain that Gainable runs for you, with private or on-prem hosting available for enterprise.

Can I build video games with Gainable?

No. Gainable is not a game engine; it generates data-backed business and internal apps such as dashboards, tables, forms, and workflows.

Can I build surveys that save responses with Gainable?

Yes. Forms and submission workflows save responses automatically to the built-in database.

Can I build multi-user web apps with Gainable?

Yes. Built-in authentication, role-based access, and real-time sync make multi-user apps the default, with unlimited free app users.

Can I share Gainable projects or apps easily?

Easy. Publish gives a live shareable URL and teammates get role-based access through shared links.
